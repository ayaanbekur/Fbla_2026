# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - New Features

## 1. Post a Found Item (As a Guest - No Account Needed!)

### Steps:
1. **From any page**, click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left
2. **Click** "📦 Post Found Item"
3. **Fill in the form**:
   - 📝 Item Name (e.g., "Blue North Face Backpack")
   - 📋 Description (color, brand, condition)
   - 📍 Location found (e.g., "Cafeteria")
   - 🏫 School
   - 🖼️ Photo (optional but helpful!)
   - 🔐 **Secret Detail** (something ONLY the owner knows):
     - ✅ Engraving text
     - ✅ Lock screen wallpaper
     - ✅ Specific scratches or marks
     - ✅ Stickers or design elements
   - 📧 Your email

4. **Click** "📤 Post Found Item"
5. **Admin reviews** your post
6. **Congratulations!** Your item is now listed

### Example Secret Details:
```
"Has initials 'JM' engraved on the metal clip"
"Lock screen shows a sunset over mountains"
"Red sticker of a pizza slice on the bottom left"
"Small tear in the front pocket, bottom right"
```

## 2. Claim an Item You Lost

### Steps:
1. **From the Browse Items page**, find your item
2. **Click the "Claim" button**
3. **The claim form opens** with these sections:

#### Your Information
- Enter your name
- Enter your email

#### Why This Is Your Item
- Explain how you lost it
- Example: "I left it in the library study room on Tuesday around 3pm"

#### Proof of Ownership
- Describe identifying features
- Example: "Blue North Face with JM initials, small tear in front pocket"
- Be specific! The more details, the better

#### Secret Detail (if item has one)
- Describe what you remember about the hidden detail
- Example: If you remember it had an engraving, describe WHAT the engraving said
- This is how the owner verifies you're the real claimant!

4. **Click** "✅ Submit Claim"
5. **Admin reviews** your claim
6. **You'll get updates** via the email you provided

### Pro Tips:
- Be as detailed and specific as possible
- If you can't describe the secret detail, the admin might reject your claim
- Include any unique marks or damage you remember
- Mention when and where you lost it

---

## 3. Admin Reviewing Claims

### For Posted Items:
1. **Go to Admin Dashboard**
2. **Look for items awaiting approval**
3. **Review the details**
4. **Click "Approve" to add to browse** or "Reject" to delete

### For Claims:
1. **In Admin Dashboard, find the item**
2. **Look at pending claims**
3. **For each claim, check**:
   - ✅ Does their reason make sense?
   - ✅ Do the features they describe match the item?
   - ✅ **IMPORTANT**: Does their secret detail answer match what you wrote?

4. **If everything matches**: Approve the claim
5. **If something's wrong**: Reject and contact claimant if possible

---

## 4. What Happens After You Claim?

1. **Admin reviews** your claim (usually within 24-48 hours)
2. **You receive an email** update about the decision
3. **If approved**: 
   - Item status changes to "Claimed"
   - You're the claimant on record
   - Make arrangements with the admin/school to pick it up
4. **If rejected**:
   - Email explains why
   - You can try again with more details

## 6. Troubleshooting

### I posted an item but don't see it in browse:
- ❌ **Normal!** Items need admin approval first
- ✅ Check back in 24 hours
- ✅ Admin reviews items in their dashboard

### I tried to claim but got an error:
- ❌ Make sure you filled in ALL required fields
- ❌ Check that you described the secret detail (if applicable)
- ✅ Try filling the form again more carefully

### I don't remember the secret detail:
- ❌ This might mean it's not your item!
- ✅ Describe as much as you DO remember
- ✅ Contact the admin and explain

### I need to change my claim:
- ❌ You can't edit after submitting
- ✅ Contact the admin via "Chat with Admin"
- ✅ Ask them to review your message
